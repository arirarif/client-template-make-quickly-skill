Month 1 — Foundation & quick wins

~6 hours ALL FREE
  1. Put an inquiry form on the contact page. /contact-us/ has no form at all — only a phone and email text link. The new meta descriptions promise “check your date”, and there is nothing to check it with.
  2. Fix the contradictory prices on /packages/. “Extra camera for Ceremony” is listed at both $200 and $149 on the same page — next to copy promising no surprises.
  3. Create the DVPFL Google Business Profile — service-area business, category Wedding videography service, covering Sarasota–Tampa. Link it from footer + contact page with a review short-link. zero Google-profile links on any of the site's 20 pages.
  4. Start the couple review engine: ask at film delivery — the emotional peak — with one 7-day reminder. Template it once. the site's testimonial area is empty today.
  5. Embed 3–5 real WeddingWire/The Knot review quotes on the homepage and /packages/. HIGH a WeddingWire reviews page and The Knot listing (id 2034487) already exist and are linked from /awards/ — but not one review sentence appears on the site itself.
  6. Tier-1 citations, one clean NAP: Apple Business Connect, Bing Places, Yelp, Nextdoor, Foursquare.

Months 2–3 — Pages that catch couple searches

the main build
  1. Turn the 8 portfolio couples into real-wedding pages — title = couple + venue + city, the existing film embedded, 500–800 words, venue and planner named and linked. HIGH Tamaryn & Martin, Kendall & Frank and six more sit in the home slider with no pages behind them; venue-name pages are the proven wedding-SEO play (“[venue] wedding video” searches).
  2. Build service-area pages for the cities the homepage already names (start: Sarasota, Bradenton, St. Petersburg, Tampa). HIGH 10 cities are listed in the homepage text; the only geo URL on the whole site is one 2022 blog post.
  3. Add geo + intent to the 9 service-page titles (“Cinematic Wedding Films in Sarasota & Venice FL”), and VideoObject schema on the 10+ Vimeo embeds. MED current titles are bare “CINEMATIC WEDDING FILM – Digital Video Production FL”; the films — the site's best asset — carry no video schema at all.
  4. Restart the blog with couple-intent posts: venue roundups (“Best wedding venues in Sarasota”), planning guides, one post per new real wedding. MED last post June 2022, and 8 of the 10 old posts chase corporate “video production” buyers — not brides.
  5. Open the YouTube channel — upload the 8 films with venue-keyword titles. MED YouTube is the second-largest search engine; a videography business with zero YouTube presence is leaving its own medium unused.
  6. Once the GBP has 5+ reviews, embed a live Google-reviews widget alongside the WeddingWire quotes.

Months 3–6 — Venues, referrals & the monthly rhythm

ongoing
  1. Run the venue circuit: cut a 45–60s venue-focused reel from existing footage for every venue already in the 8 films, gift it to the events manager, then ask what it takes to join their preferred-vendor list. HIGH
  2. Build the photographer/planner/DJ referral network. HIGH the /vendors/ page already links 6 partners outward with no evidence of links back — and wedding photographers who don't offer video are the highest-volume referral source for videographers.
  3. Free wedding-platform storefronts: Zola (genuinely free, pay-per-connect), plus polishing the existing Knot/WeddingWire listings and porting Google reviews there. Hold the paid Knot/WeddingWire tiers until the free listings prove lead flow — 2026 research says they're a hold-until-proven expense. MED
  4. Ride engagement season (Dec–Feb): most couples get engaged now and book vendors for next year. Reels cadence doubles, venue posts peak, real-wedding pages and reviews must already be live — that is why Months 1–3 are front-loaded.
