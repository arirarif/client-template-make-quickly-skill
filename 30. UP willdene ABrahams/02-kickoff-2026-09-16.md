# Kickoff — Willdene hired us (2026-09-16)

Willdene chose us and sent (a) 4 milestones, (b) the end client's confirmed wishes, and (c) a request for
one complete list of what we need from the client. The client plans to have the first page's material
ready by **Saturday 2026-09-19**.

---

## 1. The whole job, in plain words

**Who is the real client?** An agency owner (Willdene's end client). His current site was made with
Hostinger and he hates it. The new site is his shop window: it has to *prove* his agency does
beautiful, high-quality digital work.

**What gets built:** a 5-page WordPress site: Home, About, Services, Portfolio, Contact.

**What he confirmed he wants:**

| Wish | What it means for the build |
|---|---|
| WordPress | Fixed. No other platform |
| Edits it himself: text, portfolio pieces, **pricing** | Everything editable in the WP editor. Portfolio = a "Case Study" post type with a simple form. Pricing = one editable pricing block/table, used wherever prices appear |
| Clean build, no unnecessary plugins | No page-builder plugin. Only a few essential plugins (forms, SEO, security/backup, SMTP) |
| No generic or off-the-shelf theme | Our own custom block theme, designed for his brand |
| Strong visual design and quality | Design is the main deliverable, not just "working pages" |
| Another developer can take it over | Standard WordPress (Gutenberg blocks, theme.json, clear file names), no locked-in builder, a short handover note |
| Changes often at this stage | Reusable block patterns so he can add sections without breaking the design |

**Builder decision:** a custom Gutenberg block theme (theme.json + block patterns + a small Case Study
post type). No Elementor, no licence. This fits "clean", "not generic" and "easy to take over" better
than Elementor Pro.

**Timeline:** 10 days, as 4 milestones (days 1–3 / 4–6 / 7–8 / 9–10).
