BNB Photos

bnbphotos.com · sells to Airbnb hosts & property managers

Strategy in one line: hosts hang out in Facebook groups and on Airbnb itself — be present in both, win “airbnb photographer + [city]” with real city pages, and land one or two property-management contracts, each worth dozens of hosts. Snowbird prep season (Sep–Nov) is this site's big window — it starts immediately, so BNB's outreach comes first.

Month 1 — Foundation & quick wins

ALL FREE
  1. Replace the home-sale copy block on the homepage. the H2 “Top-Rated Real Estate Photography… attract more buyers and faster offers” is house-sale copy on a vacation-rental site — wrong audience, verbatim on the live page.
  2. Make the footer's “a division of Express Real Estate Photos” an actual link, and get the link back from Express.
  3. Create the BNB Google Business Profile — service-area business, hidden address, same five-city coverage. Category: Photographer. Link it from footer + contact page with a “Review us on Google” short-link. zero Google-profile links on any of the site's 13 pages.
  4. Start the host review engine: ask when their listing goes live with the new photos — that is the moment hosts are happiest. Template it once.
  5. Tier-1 citations, one clean NAP: Apple Business Connect, Bing Places, Yelp, Nextdoor, Foursquare.
  6. List BNB on Airbnb's own Services marketplace (Photography category — verified live in 2026, free to list, 15% per-booking fee). HIGH a city-level photographer directory inside the platform every host already uses; BNB is absent from it.

Months 2–3 — Pages that catch host searches

the main build
  1. Create 4 city landing pages — Sarasota, North Port, Englewood, Nokomis/Siesta Key — mirroring the existing service-page template. HIGH these cities are already declared in the site's schema on all 13 pages, but have zero actual pages — schema promises what the site doesn't deliver.
  2. Build a /portfolio/ page with before/after pairs and, where a host shares it, the booking-lift story. HIGH homepage says “View Our Work” — there is no portfolio URL in the entire sitemap.
  3. Create a /pricing/ page and link the homepage package cards to it. MED the $199-base pricing lives only on the homepage; “airbnb photography cost” searches have no landing page.
  4. Turn the existing photo-prep checklist post into a gated PDF — the lead magnet hosts actually want. HIGH zero email-capture forms across all 13 pages; the checklist post already exists at 448 words.
  5. Add FAQPage schema to the FAQ blocks that already exist on the homepage and aerial page. MED
  6. Once the GBP has 5+ reviews, embed a live Google-reviews widget on the homepage.

Months 3–6 — PM contracts & the monthly rhythm

ongoing
  1. Pitch the six named Sarasota/Venice property managers — Gulf Coast PM, iTrip Sarasota & Venice, Stringer, Compass, Horse and Chaise, Global Vacation — flat per-property rate plus one free demo shoot. One PM contract equals dozens of hosts. HIGH
  2. Work the host Facebook groups (The STR Community, Hosts Helping Hosts, Florida/Suncoast groups): answer photo-critique threads weekly, one before/after case study per month, no selling — the checklist PDF is the soft close. MED
  3. Ride snowbird season (Sep–Nov): hosts refresh photos before winter bookings — heaviest outreach window of the year. GBP offer post + group case studies + PM pitches all timed here.
