Frequently Asked Questions
1. What is included in the formation package?
All packages include the essentials needed to legally form your business: preparation and filing of your formation documents, business name verification, digital delivery of approved documents, filing status updates, and secure client portal access. Our higher-tier packages — Pro and Premium — also include additional services, such as EIN filing and other startup support services, bundled at significantly lower pricing than purchased separately. Of course, clients may also select additional services within any package based on their specific business needs.
2. Which package should I choose?
• Basic – Ideal for experienced founders who only need business formation services and can handle the remaining setup steps themselves.
• Pro – Best for busy entrepreneurs who want additional setup support while still managing a few tasks independently.
• Premium – Designed for founders who want a more complete setup experience with expedited processing and broader hands-on support from our team.
3. Why are state fees separate?
State filing fees are government charges paid directly to the Secretary of State. These fees vary by state and entity type and are not included in BizJump’s service fees.
4. Do I need an EIN?
Almost all businesses require an EIN (Employer Identification Number) to open a business bank account, hire employees, process payroll, or file taxes.
5. What is a Registered Agent?
A Registered Agent receives legal notices and official government correspondence on behalf of your business.
6. Do I need a Registered Agent?
Most states legally require businesses to maintain a Registered Agent during the life of the company. You may act as your own Registered Agent by using your business address. However, if you are using your home address, your personal address information may become publicly available, which can create privacy concerns.
7. What are Compliance Alerts?
Compliance Alerts provide reminders about annual reports, filing deadlines, and important Secretary of State compliance requirements to help keep your business in good standing.
8. How fast is filing?
Estimated filing times vary based on the package selected and the processing speed of the Secretary of State. Some states also charge additional expedited processing fees, which may range from approximately $50 to $100 or more.
9. Can BizJump guarantee state approval?
No. Final approval is determined solely by the Secretary of State and depends on factors such as name availability and compliance with state requirements. If your requested business name is unavailable, we will work with you to select an alternative.
10. Do I need a business bank account?
Yes. Separating personal and business finances is strongly recommended for liability protection, accounting accuracy, and tax purposes.
11. Can BizJump help with taxes?
Yes. We offer CPA-supported tax filing services, accounting setup, bookkeeping guidance, and tax planning support.
12. Can you help set up my website and social media?
Yes. BizJump offers website setup and hosting, Google Business Profile setup, and social media business account configuration services.
13. Are monthly fees refundable?
Monthly service subscriptions may be canceled according to the terms of the service agreement. Government filing fees are generally non-refundable once submitted to the state.
14. Is BizJump a law firm?
No. BizJump is not a law firm and does not provide legal advice. All information provided is for educational and business support purposes only.
