Hijabs

Most of our hijabs are one size. Typical dimensions: chiffon and modal shawls 180 × 75 cm; jersey wraps 170 × 65 cm; instant hijabs one size, stretch fit.

How to measure

  • Bust: around the fullest part, tape level and relaxed.
  • Waist: around the narrowest part of your waist.
  • Hips: around the fullest part of your hips.
