## The message

Hi Paul,

Quick update on all three sites. Short version: they're in far better shape than a week ago, and
none of it needed anything from you.

**Your sites are now protected properly**

- All three are backed up automatically now — the database every night, the full site weekly.
  Before this, nothing was being backed up at all. If a site ever broke, it can be put back.
- Two of the three had no protection on the login page whatsoever. Someone was already trying —
  25 break-in attempts in a single week on one site. Both are now protected.
- Everything is up to date. One of the updates fixed two known security holes.
- Removed 13 old plugins that were sitting unused on your sites. Unused doesn't mean harmless —
  they're still a way in.

**Your sites are easier to find**

- Your Express homepage was showing up in Google as "Express" — the rest of your business name
  was cut off, so you looked like an unknown company in every search result. It now reads
  "Express Real Estate Photos" properly. It was also displaying a piece of broken code in the
  middle of the text. Both fixed.
- Two of the sites had duplicate pages competing with your real pages in Google. Removed.
- Added a file that lets ChatGPT and similar AI assistants describe your business correctly when
  someone asks them to recommend a photographer.

**Your photos now work for you**

Every image across Express and BNB Photos — nearly 400 of them — now has a proper description
attached. Two things come from that: Google can actually find your photos in image search, and
people using screen readers can use your sites.

On the wedding site this mattered more than I expected. Your awards page has The Knot Best of
Weddings for 2007 and 2012 through 2020, and WeddingWire Couples' Choice 2016 through 2020 — a
decade of awards that were completely invisible to Google. They aren't any more.

**What's still to come**

The big one is the Google listings, and that's the ten minutes I still need from you. Everything
above helps people who already found you. The Google listing is what gets you found in the first
place — the map that appears above all the website results.

Two smaller things I'll finish after that: your mailing list, and one technical fix on BNB Photos
that's causing the indexing emails Google keeps sending you.

Send me a couple of times that suit you and we'll get the listings done.

Thanks,
Arif
