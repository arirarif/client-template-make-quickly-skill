Month 1 — Foundation & quick wins

~6 hours ALL FREE
  1. Fix the homepage title tag — complete it to “Real Estate Photography Venice & Sarasota FL | Express Real Estate Photos”. the live title literally ends at “| Express” — the brand is truncated in every Google result.
  2. Add Search Console. GA4 exists; queries and rankings are invisible without it. 20 minutes, and it sets the baseline every later number is judged against.
  3. Fix the /book-now/ sitemap entry. that URL serves a 27-word “Vercel Security Checkpoint” challenge to Google while sitting in the sitemap — Google may index an error page as your booking page.
  4. Link BNB Photos properly from the footer (and get the link back — BNB's footer names Express with no link today). An aged domain's link is free authority, both ways.
  5. Create the Express Google Business Profile — service-area business, hidden address, covering Venice, Sarasota, North Port, Bradenton, Port Charlotte. Category: Real estate photographer. Fill every field; use the existing booking app as the GBP booking link. Link it from footer + contact page. zero google.com/maps, g.page or business.google links on any of the site's 16 pages.
  6. Start the agent review engine: ask after the 2nd–3rd completed shoot (agents review once they trust you), via Google's review short-link. Template the email once — 2 minutes per job after that. Never gate, never incentivize (2026 Google policy). homepage testimonials are anonymous initials with broken characters (“Michael R. �definitely…”) — real Google reviews replace them.
  7. Tier-1 citations, one clean NAP: Apple Business Connect, Bing Places, Yelp, Nextdoor, Foursquare — about an hour total.

Months 2–3 — Pages that catch agent searches

the main build
  1. Clone the proven service-page template into 5–7 service-area pages: Nokomis, North Port, Osprey, Englewood, Siesta Key, Lakewood Ranch, Port Charlotte. HIGH those towns appear zero times across all 16 pages — “real estate photographer North Port” is currently uncontested by this site.
  2. Switch page schema from Article to LocalBusiness + Service in the already-installed Rank Math, with areaServed, phone and the visible $149–$449 price range. HIGH the site currently tells Google it publishes articles, not that it is a local photography business.
  3. Cross-link the 8 service pages to each other and to the blog. HIGH every service page links only to home/about/contact — the photography page never links floor plans, aerial, or the “sells homes faster” post.
  4. Build 2–3 case-study pages from the existing #our-work galleries: property, package used, days on market, agent quote — linkable proof for the RE/MAX and KW logos already on the site. MED
  5. Gate the best blog post as an agent checklist PDF (“prepare your listing for the photoshoot”) with an email form. MED no email capture exists anywhere on the site; the 903-word prep post is already written.
  6. Once the GBP has 5+ reviews, embed a live Google-reviews widget on the homepage, replacing the anonymous quotes.

Months 3–6 — Repeat business & the monthly rhythm

ongoing
  1. Agent retention email: monthly one-email digest to past agents (new work, seasonal reminders, referral ask). Repeat agents are the whole business model. HIGH
  2. Pitch brokerage vendor lists: the RE/MAX and KW offices already represented in the portfolio — ask the office admin what it takes to be on the recommended-vendor sheet, case-study pages in hand. HIGH
  3. Time the spring push: January–April is Sarasota listing season. Have all area pages indexed, reviews visible and the digest running before January. Optional in this window: a Google Local Services Ads test (pay-per-lead, ~$25–$50/lead) — only after reviews exist.
