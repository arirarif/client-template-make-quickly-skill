# 13 — Traffic opportunities, measured

Not a generic SEO checklist. Every item below comes from crawling the three sites on 2026-09-09:
word counts, internal-link counts, navigation structure and live search results.

**Context:** the technical work is done. All three sites are updated, protected, correctly
titled, free of duplicate archives, and two of the three have zero images missing alt text.
Nothing technical is holding them back any more. What is left is **presence and content**.

---

## The measurements

**Content depth — money pages are thin**

| Page | Express | BNB |
|---|---|---|
| Contact | 343 words | **223 words** |
| About | 324 | 295 |
| Services | 511 | 501 |
| Book Now | — | 334 |
| Service pages (photography, aerial, video, floor plans) | 642–841 | 719–846 |
| Homepage | 1,164 | 1,609 |

The individual service pages are healthy. The **hub pages are not** — a 501-word "Services" page
is a menu, not a page that can rank for anything.

**Internal linking — the blog is nearly invisible**

- Blog posts receive **4 internal links each** on both sites, all from a homepage block.
- **Neither Express nor BNB has a blog index page or a "Blog" nav item.** DVPFL does, and should
  be the model.
- Category archives receive **zero** internal links. They exist, they are indexed, nothing points
  at them.

Three well-written posts per site (425–903 words) are doing almost no work because almost nothing
links to them.

**Competitive position**

Searching Paul's own brand name returns a **competitor's paid ad above his website**
(`anyhomeshoots.com`). He has no listing to outrank it with.

## Options, ranked by what actually moves traffic

### 1. Location pages — the largest organic gain available
**Unblocked · outside the $300 · roughly 9 pages**

Nobody searches "vacation rental photographer". They search **"Airbnb photographer Siesta Key"**.
Neither site has a page containing those words, so neither can rank for them.

- **BNB:** Siesta Key · Anna Maria Island · Venice · Englewood · Nokomis
- **Express:** Sarasota · North Port · Bradenton · Port Charlotte

Written the way the DVPFL service pages were in August: grounded entirely in facts already on his
sites, nothing invented. Each page targets a search that exists and currently returns competitors.

**This is real content work and should be quoted separately.** Absorbing nine pages into a $300
maintenance job means doing the most valuable work of the engagement for nothing.

### 2. Give the blog a home
**Unblocked · a few hours · in the spirit of the current job**

Create a blog index page on Express and BNB, add it to the navigation, and link relevant posts
from the matching service pages — "How to Prepare Your Property" belongs on the photography page,
"Matterport vs Zillow" belongs on both tour pages.

Costs nothing, uses content that already exists, and gives future posts somewhere to live. Right
now every post Paul ever publishes lands in a place nobody links to.

### 3. Cross-link Express and BNB
**Unblocked · one hour**

BNB's footer **names** Express with no link. Two aged domains in the same market, passing no
authority to each other, and no path for a visitor who needs the other service. Link both ways.

### 4. Thicken the four hub pages
**Unblocked · half a day**

Contact, About and Services on both sites. About pages especially: 300 words cannot carry
thirty years of experience, the awards, or the service area — all of which are ranking signals
and trust signals at the same time.

### 5. FAQ sections with FAQ schema
**Unblocked on Express and DVPFL · blocked on BNB by the Wordfence issue**

Hosts and agents ask the same questions: how many photos, do you stage, Airbnb image limits, is
twilight worth it, how fast is delivery. Answering them on-page captures long-tail searches and
can earn expanded search results. Writable from existing content.

### 6. Move the three Uncategorized posts into real categories
**Unblocked · ten minutes** — Express only. Small, but they are real posts sitting in a junk bucket.

### 7. Publishing cadence
**Needs a budget decision**

Three posts per site, all from May 2026, then silence. DVPFL's blog has been dead since June 2022.
Search rewards sites that stay active. Two posts a month per site is the standard recommendation;
one a month would still beat what is happening now.

## What will not help

More technical audits. Another pass of alt text. Micro-tuning titles that are already correct.
That work is finished, and repeating it produces reports rather than visitors.

## The honest ordering

The **Google Business Profiles still outrank everything on this list** — they are free, they sit
above all website results, and all three brands are absent. Ten minutes from Paul beats every item
here.

But items 2, 3, 4, 5 and 6 need nobody's permission and can start today. Item 1 needs a price.
