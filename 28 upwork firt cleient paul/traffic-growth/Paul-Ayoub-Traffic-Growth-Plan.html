Cost summary

Almost everything in all three plans is free — the spend is labor, not media. Optional inside the 6 months: Google Local Services Ads test for Express (pay-per-lead, ~$25–$50/lead, only after reviews exist) and the paid Knot/WeddingWire tiers only if their free listings prove lead flow first. Meta ads: not recommended at this budget in 2026 (benchmarks: ~$45/lead and ~$50/day minimum for the algorithm) — the pixel gets installed now, ads wait.
