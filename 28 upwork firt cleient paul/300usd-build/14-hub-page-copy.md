# 14 — Hub page copy (paste-ready)

For the four thin pages measured in file 13. Every fact below is taken from Paul's own sites —
nothing invented. Anything I could not verify is marked `[CONFIRM]`.

**How to use:** open the page in Elementor, click the text widget, replace the copy. No layout
changes needed. Roughly an hour for all four.

---

## First, a defect worth fixing while you are in there

**BNB Photos' About page is Express's copy.** On a vacation-rental site it currently says:

> "I'm Paul, the owner of **Digital Video Productions and Express Real Estate Photos**… helps your
> **listings stand out and sell faster**."

Two problems. It names the wrong brands to a host who has never heard of them, and it promises the
wrong outcome — a host does not want a sale, they want **bookings**. The same paste is why the
page reads as generic. The rewrite below fixes both.

---

## Why this is worth the hour

These four pages are the ones a serious buyer reads last, right before deciding. They are also the
thinnest pages on both sites — a 223-word Contact page cannot rank for anything and does not answer
the questions that stop someone booking. The rewrites do three things at once: give Google
something to index, answer the objections, and put the phone number and service area on the pages
where intent is highest.
