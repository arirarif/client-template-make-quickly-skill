### Returns & Exchanges Policy (client's final text — post verbatim)

At Niswah Boutique, we want you to be happy with your purchase. Please review the following policy before placing your order.

#### 1. Returns for Change of Mind
Eligible items may be requested for return or exchange within 3 days from the date of delivery, provided that:
- The item is unused, unworn and in its original condition.
- All original tags, labels and packaging are intact.
- The item has not been washed, altered, damaged or used.
- The item is returned with the original invoice/order details.

Return or exchange requests that do not meet these conditions may be rejected.

#### 2. Wrong, Defective or Damaged Items
If you receive an item that:
- Is different from the item you ordered;
- Does not match the description or specifications provided on the website;
- Has a manufacturing defect; or
- Was damaged before or during delivery,

please contact us as soon as possible after receiving the order.

Niswah Boutique will assess the issue and, where applicable, provide an appropriate remedy in accordance with applicable Qatar consumer protection requirements, which may include replacement, repair or refund.

For defective, damaged, incorrect or non-conforming products, Niswah Boutique will not charge the customer for the applicable return/replacement process where the issue is attributable to Niswah Boutique or the product.

Customers may be requested to provide photographs or other information to help us verify the issue.

#### 3. Hygiene-Sensitive Products
For hygiene and health reasons, the following items are not eligible for return or exchange due to change of mind, provided they are correctly supplied, free from defects and conform to their description:
- Hijabs
- Hijab Caps / Undercaps
- Jewellery
- Accessories
- Other products identified on the product page as hygiene-sensitive

This restriction does not affect any rights the customer may have under applicable Qatar law where a product is defective, damaged, incorrect or does not conform to the agreed description or specifications.

#### 4. Sale & Promotional Items
Sale, discounted and promotional items are generally final sale and are not eligible for return or exchange due to change of mind, unless otherwise stated on the product page.

However, this does not exclude or limit any consumer rights or remedies that apply under Qatar law where an item is defective, damaged, incorrect or does not conform to the description or agreed specifications.

#### 5. Exchanges – Size or Colour
Where an item is eligible for exchange, customers may request an alternative available size or colour.

Exchange is subject to:
- Product availability;
- The item meeting the return conditions; and
- Applicable delivery/collection charges, where the exchange is requested due to customer preference or change of mind.

Where the exchange is required because Niswah Boutique supplied an incorrect, defective, damaged or non-conforming item, the applicable return/replacement costs will be handled by Niswah Boutique, subject to verification.

#### 6. How to Request a Return or Exchange
Please email us at: info@niswahboutique.com with:
- Order number
- Customer name and contact number
- Reason for return/exchange
- Photos, where applicable

Our team will review the request and guide you through the next steps.

Please do not send any item back before receiving confirmation from Niswah Boutique.

#### 7. Return Shipping & Delivery Charges
For customer-requested returns or exchanges due to change of mind, applicable return, collection and/or delivery charges may apply.

Where the return is due to an incorrect, defective, damaged or non-conforming product attributable to Niswah Boutique, the applicable return/replacement costs will be covered by Niswah Boutique, subject to verification.

#### 8. Refunds
Once the returned item has been received and inspected, we will confirm whether the return qualifies for a refund.

Where a refund is approved, it will be processed through the applicable payment method, subject to the payment method used and applicable processing procedures.
- **Cash on Delivery (COD):** Refund may be provided through an approved refund method or Niswah Boutique credit/voucher, as applicable and agreed with the customer.
- **Online/Card Payment:** Refund will generally be processed through the original payment method, subject to payment gateway/bank processing times.
- **Store Credit/Voucher:** Where applicable, an approved refund may be issued as Niswah Boutique credit/voucher.

Any refund will be handled in accordance with applicable Qatar consumer protection requirements.

#### 9. Important Notice
Nothing in this Returns & Exchanges Policy is intended to exclude, restrict or waive any mandatory rights or remedies available to consumers under applicable laws and regulations of the State of Qatar.

Niswah Boutique reserves the right to verify the condition and reason for any return or exchange request before approving it.

For assistance:
Email: info@niswahboutique.com
Instagram: @boutiqueniswah

### 0. Ground rules that apply to every phase

1. **Never edit layouts inside Divi.** Header, footer, Home, Collections are generated by
   `niswah-boutique/build/theme-builder.build.mjs`; the next "Import Everything" overwrites
   builder edits. Change the generator, the CSS, or `functions.php`.
2. **Ship loop, every phase:**
   ```
   bump   Version: in style.css  ==  NB_THEME_VERSION in functions.php
   node   niswah-boutique/build/theme-builder.build.mjs
   pwsh   niswah-boutique/build/pack.ps1
   WP     Appearance -> Add Theme -> Upload -> Replace installed with uploaded
   WP     Appearance -> Niswah Starter -> Import Everything      <- the step people forget
   check  in the real browser (colours, hover, mobile 400px)
   git    commit scoped to "29 UP Arif/"   (user pushes)
   ```
3. **Disk is nearly full on the server.** First upload of this round will probably fail with
   "Failed to write file to disk" — see Phase 0.
4. **Do not reset Divi Theme Builder. Do not install anything from `DIVI/`** (nulled).
5. **Test CSS by injecting FIRST in `<head>`** before trusting it — Divi TB / divi-dynamic /
   woocommerce-smallscreen load after the child theme and win ties on specificity.
6. One phase at a time. Each phase ends with a live check and a commit.

### Phase 5 — EN / AR bilingual with RTL (~6–8 h, the big one)

#### 5.1 Plugin decision -> **TranslatePress (free)**

Why not the others: Polylang/WPML duplicate every page and product — with generated
layouts and an importer that rebuilds pages, duplicates are a maintenance trap.
TranslatePress translates the *rendered* page in place, works with Divi shortcodes and
WooCommerce, ships a `[language-switcher]` shortcode, and sets `dir="rtl"` on `<html>`
for Arabic automatically. Free tier = one extra language, which is exactly this job.

Trade-off to tell the client: automatic machine translation needs a Google Translate or
DeepL API key (their account, small cost); otherwise every string is translated by hand in
the TranslatePress editor. **Plan: install, enable Arabic, wire the switcher and RTL CSS,
hand-translate the UI chrome (nav, header, footer, cart/checkout, buttons, policy page
headings) — product descriptions are machine-translated only if the client supplies a key.**
Ask the client for the key up front.

#### 5.2 Switcher in the header, every page

Generator `brandSection`: add a Code module on the right of the icon row (before search)
containing `[language-switcher]`; TranslatePress option "flags off, short names EN | AR".
Style in `style.css` §4 to match the nav (Jost 300, 13px, letter-spacing). Mobile: put it
inside the mobile menu panel, top line. Because it lives in the global header it is on
product, cart, checkout, and every legal page — the client's explicit ask.

#### 5.3 RTL CSS — new `style.css` §13 "RTL"

Divi core handles most flips when `dir="rtl"`. Child-theme rules to mirror explicitly:

- header icon row (absolute-positioned, pixel-measured) -> mirror `left`/`right`
- product card top-right badge / quick-view -> top-left
- cart two-column and checkout two-column order (`flex-direction: row-reverse`)
- price superscript decimals (`nb_price_decimals_superscript`) — keep LTR numerals inside
  `<bdi>`; QAR sign already renders as "ر.ق"
- sticky gallery rail moves to the right of the image
- footer column order; text-align right in `.nb-footer`
- Arabic font: `nb_arabic_font_css()` already enqueues one — verify which family and apply
  it on `html[dir=rtl] body` for body and headings (Marcellus has no Arabic glyphs)

#### 5.4 Translate the chrome

Home/Collections/About), search/cart labels, footer, all buttons, cart/checkout field
