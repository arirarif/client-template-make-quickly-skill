Niswah Boutique is a modest fashion house based in Doha, Qatar. We design and curate hijabs, abayas, jilbabs and everyday pieces for women who want to look composed without compromising on comfort or coverage.

Our story

Niswah began with a simple frustration: finding modest clothing that felt considered from the first wear. Fabric that holds its shape through a long day. Colour that stays true after washing. A finish that looks as good at the office as it does at a family gathering. We started with a small edit of hijabs and have grown into a full wardrobe, one carefully chosen piece at a time.

What we stand for

  • Quality first. Every fabric is handled and worn before it reaches the shop. If it does not pass a full day on us, it does not get listed.
  • Quiet luxury. Clean lines, calm colours and details that reward a second look, without logos or noise.
  • Made for real life. Breathable weights for the Gulf climate, generous cuts, and sizes that are honest.
  • Service you can reach. A real person answers our email and Instagram messages, in English or Arabic.
