## The insight that leads the letter

**The portfolio is the sales page for a new agency with no history of its own.** So it must be a
real system, not a gallery:
- **Case Study post type with fixed fields:** client/industry, services used (tags: SEO, Web Dev…),
  challenge, solution, results (numbers), testimonial, screenshots, live link.
- **Filter by service**, so a visitor who wants SEO sees SEO proof.
- Each case study has **its own page** (SEO + shareable in sales emails).
- Adding a new project = filling a form. The layout stays locked.
- **Service pages link to matching case studies automatically** (same service tag). Proof sits next
  to the offer.

  agree beforehand whether it's unpaid or a small fee ($100).
- **AI Solutions / Email Automation** are only services listed on the site, not features you build.
  Chatbots or email integrations = separate quote.
