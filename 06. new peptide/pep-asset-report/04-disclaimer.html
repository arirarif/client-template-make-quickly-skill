Step-by-step · How to wire it in

Deployment Recipe.

  1. Upload pep/04.png to WP Media Library. Note its attachment ID.
  2. Open 02-woodmart-child/functions.php and the pp_atomic_pdp_brand_images() hook will append both assets (01 + 04) to the product gallery automatically.
  3. Replace the placeholder $disclaimer_id = 1235; with the real attachment ID.
  4. Visit any product page → confirm the disclaimer appears as image #3 in the gallery.
  5. Recommended extra: Add an <img> tag at the bottom of every product short description (via Woo's woocommerce_short_description filter) for extra prominence.
