# 10 — Access request message (send this one)

**Replaces file 01.** Same asks, but built around a single 15-minute call instead of four
separate homework items, because Paul is not technical and said plainly he does not want to
spend time on this.

**Why a call rather than instructions:** every one of these needs either his Google login or a
code Google sends only to him. Written instructions would mean four rounds of back-and-forth and
him learning things he does not want to learn. On a call he clicks "next" a few times, reads a
code aloud, and it is finished.

**No jargon in the message.** No "GBP", "schema", "Brevo", "opt-in". He does not need those words
to say yes.

---

## The message

Hi Paul,

Quick update, then one small ask.

Everything on your three sites is now backed up, up to date and running clean — I've been through
all of them this week and fixed the bits that were quietly hurting you in Google. Nothing needs
your attention there.

There's one thing left that I genuinely can't do without you, and it's the biggest one.

**None of your three businesses has a Google listing.** That's the box that appears on the right
of the screen — with the map, your phone number, your photos and your reviews — when someone
Googles you. It's also what puts you in the little map of three businesses that shows up *above*
all the website results when someone searches "real estate photographer near me". It's free, and
right now all three of your businesses are invisible there.

I can build all three. The catch is that Google verifies the owner, not me — it either mails a
postcard to you or rings the number on the listing, and only you can pick that up.

**So: can we do 15 minutes on a call?** I'll share my screen and do the typing. You just click
"next" a few times and read me the code when Google sends it. In that same 15 minutes I can also
grab the last few details I need, so this is the only time I have to bother you.

Have these handy if you can:

- Your opening hours for each business
- Roughly how fast you deliver photos to agents (next day? two days?)
- Whether you still offer the 3D Matterport tours
- 3–5 emails or texts from happy clients — forward them any time, before or after

Send me a couple of times that work and I'll fit around you. Evening is fine.

Thanks,
Arif

## Notes for me (do not send)

**What the call has to accomplish, in order — the whole point is not needing a second call:**

1. **Create all three Google listings.** Content is already written and ready to paste from
   `08-gbp-content-pack.md` — categories, service areas, descriptions, services, first posts.
   Do not improvise on the call; read from the file.
2. **Start verification on all three.** Postcard or phone. Gate G11 is "submitted", not
   "verified" — Google owns the timing after that.
3. **Get the five facts** — hours ×3, Express turnaround, Matterport yes/no, opening years.
   These are the `[bracketed]` blanks in files 06 and 08. Ask them while he is on the line;
   chasing them by email is how this stalls for a month.
4. **Get the email address for the mailing list account.** Frame it as "which email address
   should the list live under, so it's yours and not mine" — he does not need to hear the word
   Brevo. That single answer unblocks the signup forms, the welcome emails and the campaigns.
5. **Elementor Pro licence on dvpfl.com.** Mention it only if there is time and he seems relaxed.
   It is his purchase; the site works without it, but the plugin is falling behind and the gap
   widens with each release. Do not lead with it — it sounds like a bill, and it is not the point
   of the call.

**Deliberately left out of the message:** the placeholder-code problem behind his Google emails,
the page-weight issue, the two backup plugins on Express, LiteSpeed. All real, none of them his
to decide, and naming them would make the call sound like a list of problems rather than a
15-minute win.

**If he says no to a call:** fall back to asking him to add me as a Manager on a Google account —
but expect that to need its own explanation, and expect the five facts to arrive slowly or not at
all. The call is worth pushing for once.
