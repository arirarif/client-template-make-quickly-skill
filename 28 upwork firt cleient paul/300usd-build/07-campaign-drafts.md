# 07 — Seasonal campaign drafts

Three campaigns, saved in Brevo as **drafts**. Never scheduled — sending is Paul's decision.
The point of writing them now: a future campaign costs him one word, not an afternoon.

Gate G8 checks all three exist as drafts.

**Why these three seasons** (from the site crawls and the growth research, not invented):
Express sells into the **spring listing season**, which starts being booked in December–January.
BNB's hosts refresh listings before **snowbird season** arrives in autumn. DVPFL's couples get
engaged over the holidays — **engagement season** runs roughly Thanksgiving to Valentine's.

Same rules as the welcome emails: plain text, one link, no fake urgency, `[brackets]` are blanks
Paul must fill.

---

## How Paul sends one (goes in the handover)

1. Brevo → Campaigns → find the draft
2. Read it, change anything that isn't true any more
3. Recipients → pick the one list the draft names
4. Send a test to himself first
5. Send

That's it. No design work, no writing, no scheduling to learn.
