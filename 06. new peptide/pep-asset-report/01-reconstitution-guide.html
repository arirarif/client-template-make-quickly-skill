Anatomy · 6 reconstitution steps

What's Inside.

Step Title What it shows Purpose
1 GEAR UP 3ml syringe · 18-gauge needle · bacteriostatic water · alcohol pads Checklist of supplies the researcher needs before starting
2 LOCK NEEDLE Hand twisting needle onto syringe via Luer-lock mechanism Secure needle attachment before removing the cap
3 STERILISE Vial caps removed, rubber stoppers wiped with alcohol pad Prevent contamination of vial contents
4 INJECT AIR Air injected into water vial before drawing liquid Equalises pressure → makes water draw out easily
5 SIDE INJECTION Water injected against the side of the peptide vial Prevents damaging the delicate lyophilised powder
6 SWIRL ONLY Gentle 4-minute swirl + fridge icon Once activated the peptide must be refrigerated

Step-by-step · How to wire it in

Deployment Recipe.

  1. Upload pep/01.png to WP Media Library. Note its attachment ID (e.g. 1234).
  2. Open 02-woodmart-child/functions.php and add the pp_atomic_pdp_brand_images() hook from the deployment plan.
  3. Replace the placeholder $reconstitution_id = 1234; with the real attachment ID from step 1.
  4. Visit any product page → confirm the guide appears as image #2 in the gallery, after the product photo.
  5. Optional: Create standalone page at /reconstitution-guide/ with the image full-width + the 6-step text below for SEO.
