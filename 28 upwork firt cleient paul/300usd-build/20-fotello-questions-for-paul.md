# 20 — Questions for Paul to ask Fotello before signing up

**Context (call, 2026-09-17, ~21:57):** Fotello emailed Paul about its website builder. He ran
its free Business Report and scored **29/100**. Fotello's editor built only a homepage draft, and
clicking Publish makes him book an appointment with them first. He is booking that appointment
and will report back. He asked us to **keep working on BNB Photos**, because Fotello is for real
estate only and won't replace that site.

Agreed on the call: don't publish yet; ask the questions below first; test on a separate domain
before switching; the Google listings still have to be done either way.

**Express work is on hold until he decides.** The Book Now link on Express
(`homes.expressrealestatephotos.com`) is his own booking app on Vercel, not Fotello.

---

## The message

Hi Paul,

Thanks for the call. Here are the questions we talked about, so you have them in front of you for
your Fotello appointment. Please don't press Publish until you have these answers.

**Your current website**
1. I already have a website, expressrealestatephotos.com, with about 17 pages. What happens to it?
2. Will you move all of those pages over, or only build a new homepage? Who writes the rest?
3. Do my blog posts come across too?

**Your domain and email**
4. Does expressrealestatephotos.com stay registered in my name?
5. How do you connect my domain? Does it move to your servers?
6. Will my email addresses on that domain keep working after the switch?

**Google**
7. Google already knows my current site. If the page addresses change, will you set up redirects
   so I don't lose my place in Google?
8. Can I test your website on a different domain first, and compare it before switching?
9. Do you set up and manage my Google Business Profile, or only score it?

**The report**
10. My score is 29 out of 100. What exactly is being measured, and what would change it?
11. Can you show me real photographers in my area whose Fotello sites rank well on Google?

**Booking and payments**
12. How do booking, pricing and payments work? Would they replace the booking page I use now?

**Cost**
13. What is the total price, monthly or yearly? What is free and what is paid?
14. Is there a contract, and how do I cancel?

**If I leave later**
15. If I stop using Fotello in a year or two, can I take my website, pages and photos with me,
    for example back to WordPress?
16. What happens to my domain, my pages and my booking history if I cancel?

Send me their answers and I'll tell you honestly whether it makes sense. In the meantime I'll keep
working on BNB Photos.

Thanks,
Arif
